Pair Glow support

Pair Glow puts a widget on your home screen that you share with one other person: tap yours, and theirs glows in your colour. It's made by KGWorks.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: write to askkgworks@gmail.com. Say which phone you have (iPhone or Android) and what you expected to see.

Pairing

  1. You both install Pair Glow and choose a name and a glow colour.
  2. One of you taps Show my code. The code works for 10 minutes and only once.
  3. The other taps Enter their code and types it, or opens the shared link.

An iPhone and an Android phone can pair with each other. You can be paired with one person at a time: pairing with someone new ends the old pair.

Adding the widget

  • iPhone: touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Pair Glow. There's also a Lock Screen widget.
  • Android: touch and hold an empty spot on the home screen, tap Widgets, find Pair Glow, and drag it into place.

Tap the glow on the widget to send one without opening the app.

A glow didn't arrive

  • Check that both phones are online. The app says so when it can't reach Pair Glow.
  • iPhone: iOS decides when to refresh widgets and may wait a little, especially in Low Power Mode. Allow Pair Glow's notifications (Settings → Notifications → Pair Glow): a glow notification lights the widget straight away.
  • Android: allow background use (Settings → Apps → Pair Glow → Battery → Unrestricted). When Pair Glow is set to Restricted, Android doesn't deliver glows until you open the app.
  • Opening Pair Glow always shows the latest glow.

Your data

  • Unpair: Settings → Unpair. Both widgets go dark.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete my data removes everything about you from Pair Glow's server and from your phone.
  • See the privacy policy for what's kept and why.
