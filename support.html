Pocket Hug support

Pocket Hug puts two little buddies on your home screen, yours and your person's. Tap yours, and on their phone your buddy gives theirs a hug, in your colour. It's made by KGWorks.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: write to askkgworks@gmail.com. Say which phone you have (iPhone or Android) and what you expected to see.

Pairing

  1. You both install Pocket Hug and choose a name and your buddy's colour.
  2. One of you taps Show my code. The code works for 10 minutes and only once.
  3. The other taps Enter their code and types it, or opens the shared link.

An iPhone and an Android phone can pair with each other. You can be paired with one person at a time: pairing with someone new ends the old pair.

Adding the widget

  • iPhone: touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Pocket Hug. There's also a Lock Screen widget.
  • Android: touch and hold an empty spot on the home screen, tap Widgets, find Pocket Hug, and drag it into place.

Tap the buddies on the widget to send a hug without opening the app.

A hug didn't arrive

  • Check that both phones are online. The app says so when it can't reach Pocket Hug.
  • iPhone: iOS decides when to refresh widgets and may wait a little, especially in Low Power Mode. Allow Pocket Hug's notifications (Settings → Notifications → Pocket Hug): a hug notification updates the widget straight away.
  • Android: allow background use (Settings → Apps → Pocket Hug → Battery → Unrestricted). When Pocket Hug is set to Restricted, Android doesn't deliver hugs until you open the app.
  • Opening Pocket Hug always shows the latest hug.

Your data

  • Stop hugging: Settings → Stop hugging with… ends the pair. Both buddies go back to their own corners.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete my data removes everything about you from Pocket Hug's server and from your phone.
  • See the privacy policy for what's kept and why.
